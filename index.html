Our Process

From Offering to Essence

Our proposed model focuses on responsible collection, processing, and extraction of discarded flowers to create valuable fragrance ingredients.

01

Collection

Partner with temples and churches to collect segregated flower waste.

02

Pre-processing

Sort, clean, and prepare the collected flowers for processing.

03

Extraction

Explore suitable methods such as steam or hydro distillation depending on the flower and desired product.

04

Quality & Fulfilment

Develop suitable testing, packaging, storage, and distribution processes as the venture grows.

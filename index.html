From Offering to Essence

Our proposed model focuses on responsible collection, processing, and extraction of discarded flowers to create valuable fragrance ingredients.

1 · Collection

Partner with temples and churches to collect segregated flower waste.

2 · Pre-processing

Sort, clean, and prepare collected flowers for further processing.

3 · Extraction

Explore suitable extraction methods such as steam or hydro distillation depending on the flower and desired product.

4 · Quality & Fulfilment

Develop appropriate quality testing, packaging, storage, and distribution processes as the venture grows.
